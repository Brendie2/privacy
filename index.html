Privacy Policy

How I handle your information.

Plain words, no tricks. This explains what I collect on this website and in the Why-Hire-You Scorecard, why I collect it, who helps me handle it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who I am

I’m Brenda Blanche, a Market Perception Strategist based in Cameroon. This policy covers brendablanche.site and the Why-Hire-You Scorecard. When I say “I” or “me,” I mean Brenda Blanche. I decide how and why your information is used.

2. What I collect

When you take the Scorecard, I collect:

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Your answers: the situation you picked first, your answers to the 18 scored questions, and your answers about whether you could deliver your service now and what kind of help you want.
  • Your scores and results.
  • Anything you choose to add in the optional boxes: the kind of work you do, the service you want to sell, who you want to work with, and the question you most want answered, in your own words.
  • Whether you agreed to receive follow-up emails.

When you message me (for example on WhatsApp or by replying to an email), I keep what you send me. If you book a Clarity Call or the Blueprint, the booking page may collect more details through its own tools.

Technical data. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages can see technical details such as your IP address and browser type. These pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google when a page opens.

3. Why I use it

  • To show and email you your results. I use your name, email and answers to build your results and send you the results email.
  • To understand your situation. I read what people add in the optional boxes to see where they are stuck, so I can answer better and improve the Scorecard.
  • To send follow-up emails, only if you ticked the optional box. These are practical emails about explaining what you do, saying your price with confidence, and getting the right clients.
  • To reply to you when you message me.

I rely on your consent for these uses. You can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who else handles your information

I use a few services to run this. They handle your information only to provide their service to me:

  • Brevo stores your contact details and Scorecard answers and sends emails.
  • Resend delivers your results email and the notification I receive when someone completes the Scorecard.
  • Cloudflare hosts these pages and processes the Scorecard form.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on these pages.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message me there.

I don’t sell your information, and I don’t give it to anyone to advertise to you. These services may process information in countries other than Cameroon.

5. Your results link

The link in your results email contains your first name and your answers in its web address, so your results page can open again. Anyone who has that link can see your results. If you want them private, don’t share the link or forward the email.

6. Cookies

These pages don’t set advertising or tracking cookies.

7. How long I keep it

I keep your information for as long as I need it for the reasons above, or until you ask me to delete it. If you unsubscribe, I stop sending follow-up emails, and I may keep a record that you unsubscribed so I don’t email you again by mistake.

8. Your choices

You can ask me to:

  • Show you the information I hold about you.
  • Correct anything that’s wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop using it for a purpose you no longer agree to.

You can unsubscribe from follow-up emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email I send.

9. Children

The Scorecard is made for working adults and isn’t meant for children. If you believe a child has given me their information, tell me and I’ll delete it.

10. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, and I only use services that are built to protect it. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so I can’t promise absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your information, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.

12. Contact me

For any question about your information, or to use any of your choices above, message me on WhatsApp or reply to any email I’ve sent you.
